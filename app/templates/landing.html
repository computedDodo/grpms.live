What You Get

Everything Your School Needs

From enrollment to graduation — GRPMS handles the full academic lifecycle with role-based access so everyone only sees what they need.

Student Management
Manual or CSV bulk enrollment. Auto-generated admission numbers with your school's prefix. Edit, archive, and restore with a tap.
Score Entry
Teachers enter CA1, CA2, Assignments, and Exams per student. Totals compute live on screen. Section-scoped subjects mean JSS and SSS students see only their relevant subjects.
Report Cards & Transcripts
Branded PDF report cards per term, cumulative transcripts across all sessions. Signature, stamp, and logo automatically included. Print-ready in one click.
Fee Enforcement Coupons
Physical coupon codes distributed each term. Students must activate their code to view results — enforcing fee payment without blocking academic records permanently.
Result Release Control
Cashier holds or publishes results per class or per student. A two-gate system: results must be both released AND coupon activated before a student can view them.
Multi-School Platform
One Platform Admin manages multiple schools. Each school is completely isolated — no data leaks between institutions. Scale from one school to dozens without a code change.

The Process

Up and Running in Minutes

No technical knowledge required. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use GRPMS.

1
Platform Admin Sets Up the School
Add the school, create a SuperAdmin account, configure sessions and terms. Takes less than 5 minutes.
2
Admin Enrolls Students & Assigns Teachers
Upload a CSV of students or add them one by one. Assign teachers to subjects and classes — including batch allocation for parallel arms.
3
Teachers Enter Scores
Each teacher logs in and enters marks for their assigned subjects. Totals and grades compute automatically.
4
Cashier Releases Results
After fee payment, distribute coupon codes. The Cashier publishes results. Students activate their code and download their report card.
Smart Fee Enforcement

Results Locked Until Fees Are Paid

Each term, Platform Admin generates unique coupon codes. Physical codes are handed to students after payment. Without activating their coupon, students cannot view results — no matter what device they use.

A3KP8M7Z
